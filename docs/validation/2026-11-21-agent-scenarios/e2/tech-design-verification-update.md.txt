## 1. Problem and scope

Two evidence defects put a design claim at risk:

- `OBS-001`: Increasing the chunk size causes a test that is meant to exercise
  multiple chunks to execute only one iteration. The test still passes.
- `OBS-002`: The benchmark measures the echo path, while the design claims a
  capture-only gain.

The response is not merely to restore the old chunk size, weaken the test, or
restate the claim to match the measurement. It is to make each claim's
verification exercise the behaviour that the claim names. A revert may later be
justified by evidence, but it is not a substitute for the missing verification.

In scope:

- the coverage contract for the multi-chunk test at the configured chunk size;
- the measurement contract for the capture-only performance claim;
- the evidence states, gaps, and handoff required to correct both.

Out of scope:

- a functional diagnosis of the chunked reader or its echo mode;
- a change to the default chunk size, the echo behaviour, or any production
  interface;
- a new benchmark framework or dependency;
- any reduction of a success threshold, change of a control, or substitution
  of a cheaper metric;
- acceptance of either claim. This document proposes verification; it does not
  discharge it.

This is a design artefact, not a Terms of Reference. The input concerns
solution-space evidence for an existing design. Applicable problem-space intent
is unavailable, and inventing users, goals, or market claims to supply it would
violate the upstream contract.

## 2. Terms

- **Chunk size (`C`)**: the maximum number of bytes the reader requests in one
  iteration.
- **Iteration**: one pass of the read-and-process loop over one chunk.
- **Multi-chunk test**: a test whose fixture (`F`) spans at least two chunks at
  the configured chunk size, so the modelled iteration count is at least two.
- **Echo path (`P_echo`)**: the execution path that writes captured bytes to a
  presentation or output sink in addition to any capture destination.
- **Echo write**: a presentation write of captured bytes, performed by the echo
  path.
- **Capture-only path (`P_capture`)**: the execution path that consumes and
  captures input without the echo write.
- **Vacuous verification**: a passing check whose preconditions are not
  satisfied by the exercised case, so the check cannot detect the defect it
  claims to cover.
- **Negative control**: a deliberately invalid case or mutation that a valid
  check must reject for the intended reason.

The brief does not define "echo" or "capture-only". These are working
definitions; `Q4` in section 10 confirms them against the benchmark harness
before `VO-002` is applied. If the project uses different meanings, this
section is updated first.

## 5. Requirements and design response

`REQ-001` – A test that claims multi-chunk coverage must observe the multi-chunk
execution at the configured chunk size. It must assert or otherwise witness an
iteration count of at least two, and it must exercise a value that crosses a
chunk boundary. A larger chunk size must not silently reduce the test to a
single-chunk case.

`REQ-002` – A performance claim must name its measured execution path and
retain the workload, control, environment, measurement protocol, uncertainty,
baseline, and acceptance threshold. A gain on the echo path does not support a
capture-only claim.

`REQ-003` – A later result may not lower the threshold, change the control, or
substitute a cheaper metric. If the claim cannot be supported at the agreed
scope, mark it unsupported and return the decision to its owner.

## 6. Verification obligations

### `VO-001` – Multi-chunk coverage at the configured chunk size

- Property: For the configured chunk size `C` and fixture `F`, the reader
  executes at least two iterations, and a sentinel whose bytes cross the `C`
  boundary is reproduced exactly. In the abstract model,
  `iterations(F, C) >= 2` and `output = expected(F)`. The test records
  `|F| > C` as an explicit precondition, so a short-read artefact alone cannot
  supply the second iteration.
- Linked requirement: `REQ-001`. Affected components: the reader test harness
  and the multi-chunk test.
- Method: a parameterized test over a small set of chunk sizes and fixtures,
  with a test-only iteration counter and a whole-buffer reference oracle. The
  counter is test-only and must not create a source-API compatibility
  obligation.
- Artefact: the existing multi-chunk test, located in `EP-M0`, plus a test-only
  counter. The exact path and test name are recorded after reconnaissance.
- Discharge condition: the red run fails on the iteration assertion with
  `iterations = 1` at the approved `C`; the green run passes with
  `iterations >= 2`; the boundary sentinel matches the independent oracle; the
  negative control collapses coverage to one iteration and is rejected for that
  reason.
- Non-vacuity: exhibit a witness with `|F| > C` that reaches the second
  iteration; exercise `C` values on both sides of the fixture boundary; run the
  negative control with `C >= |F|` and observe the iteration assertion fail. An
  empty loop, a fixture that fits in one chunk, an expected-failure marker in
  the final state, or a filtered case is not evidence.
- Bounds and residual uncertainty: the check covers selected fixtures and
  chunk sizes, not every admissible `C` or short-read pattern. If the harness
  supports a generated property over `C`, that extends the domain; otherwise
  record the residual gap. The production default `C` must be exercised.

### `VO-002` – Capture-only measurement for the performance claim

- Property: The benchmark result offered for `CLM-002` is produced by the
  capture-only path, and the echo path is a separately labelled control. A
  measured statistic `T(P)` is evidence for a claim about `P` only.
- Linked requirement: `REQ-002`. Affected components: the benchmark harness and
  the design's performance claim record.
- Method: two labelled benchmark runs, capture-only and echo control, with the
  same workload, input, environment, and protocol; path verification that the
  capture-only run performs no echo write; repeated measurements with reported
  dispersion; and a pre-registered acceptance threshold. Path verification runs
  separately from the timed measurement, or uses instrumentation disabled
  during timing, so it does not perturb the result.
- Measurement record: workload, control, environment, selected path, protocol,
  uncertainty, baseline, threshold, and owner. Section 7 holds the current
  record; every unknown remains `[OPEN]` until supplied.
- Discharge condition: the harness demonstrates that the capture-only run
  executes `P_capture` and the echo run executes `P_echo`; the capture-only
  result meets the pre-registered threshold with uncertainty reported; the
  claim is restated with the measured path, workload, and environment. If the
  result does not meet the threshold, the owner withdraws or revises the claim;
  nobody rescues it with a threshold change.
- Non-vacuity: assert that the capture-only run transfers a non-zero number of
  bytes and performs zero echo writes; assert that the echo control performs
  echo writes. Include a negative control or representative mutation in the
  capture path that changes the measured result for the intended reason. A
  benchmark that skips the path, measures zero bytes, or differs only in
  unrelated startup work is rejected.
- Bounds and residual uncertainty: the result is environment- and
  workload-specific. A throughput difference does not establish a causal
  mechanism unless that mechanism is separately instrumented. The claim states
  both limits.

### `VO-003` – Evidence freshness

- Property: Any change to `C`, `F`, the benchmark path selector, the benchmark
  harness, the performance-relevant implementation, or the upstream design
  revision reopens `VO-001` and `VO-002` until they are re-run.
- Linked requirement: `REQ-003`.
- Method: a recorded trigger list, checked at each milestone and before the
  claim is accepted.
- Discharge condition: the triggers are recorded here and in the ExecPlan, and
  each change is followed by a re-run or an explicit unverified state.
- Non-vacuity: a deliberately changed chunk size in a test parameter must
  reopen the iteration-count obligation; a changed benchmark path label must
  reopen the performance claim.

## 8. Decisions

`D1` – Do not accept the passing multi-chunk test as evidence for `CLM-001`.
The test executes one iteration, so it exercises the single-chunk path. Accept
the pass only for that narrower path. Rationale: a passing check demonstrates
only the property and scope it actually exercises.

`D2` – Do not accept the echo benchmark as evidence for `CLM-002`. Rationale:
a throughput gain on one execution path is not evidence for a claim about a
different path, nor for an unmeasured causal mechanism.

`D3` – Preserve the agreed threshold, control, and workload. Do not lower a
target or substitute a cheaper metric after seeing results. If the claim fails,
return the decision to its owner.

`D4` – Reopen both obligations after any change listed in `VO-003`.

`D5` – The functional implementation is unproven, not broken. These
observations establish evidence gaps, not functional defects, unless the
required verification finds one.
