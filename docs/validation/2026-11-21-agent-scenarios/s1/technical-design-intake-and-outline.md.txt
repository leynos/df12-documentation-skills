## Contract records owed

- Conformance basis. Cite terms of reference v0.1 with its identifiers, this
  outline's agreement state, and any later brief revision. No design, ADR, or
  roadmap revision exists to cite.
- Concerns and views. Two candidate concerns come from §4 of the terms of
  reference: the sponsor's outcome question and the maintainer's support
  obligation. Choose the smallest representation that answers each; do not
  default to a diagram.
- Baseline, target, and gaps. No implementation baseline exists, so the design
  states that explicitly and records no gap register or migration programme.
  The target cannot be described until TOR-OPEN-003 to TOR-OPEN-006 resolve.
- Verification obligations. None can be named yet. That is a state, not a
  decision: requirements, invariants, lemmas, axioms, and any combinatorial
  surface are unknown. When they exist, each obligation needs a method,
  artefact, evidence, discharge condition, and a non-vacuity argument.
- Compatibility assessment. No released surface, external consumer, deployed
  data, or peer is established. Pre-1.0 source-API compatibility machinery is
  excluded by policy. If the design introduces a persisted or wire format, the
  assessment changes even though no deployed state exists today.
- Handoff and readiness. Not ready. See below.

## First design decision: technology selection

No technology is imposed (TOR-CONSTRAINT-002). The design must therefore select
and justify the language and runtime, the packaging and distribution channel,
the dependency set, and the API shape against the terms of reference's
requirements and constraints. It must not present a preference as a constraint,
and this outline proposes none.

The choice is gated by TOR-OPEN-002 (caller ecosystem) and TOR-OPEN-006
(environmental constraints). Research into alternatives cannot be targeted
before both resolve; a survey conducted now would be a literature review with
no decision to inform.

## Research status and gaps

- Ecosystem survey: not performed. No design-relevant question is answerable
  without the caller ecosystem and the capability set.
- Standards and specifications: unknown; they depend on the domain.
- Technology baseline: deferred to the selected ecosystem. Version claims must
  be current when drafting starts, not inherited from this note.
- Firecrawl and web research were not used. The gap is recorded rather than
  filled with generalities.

## What would change this outline

- Multiple actors, lifecycle behaviour, or an integration path: add the
  scenario probe to the terms of reference, a state-machine view, and
  ordering or liveness obligations.
- An imposed platform or distribution mandate: record it as an upstream
  constraint and constrain the design options accordingly.
- Persistence or a wire protocol: add data-model, migration, and deployment
  records.
- A published API with external consumers: release lineage and a compatibility
  commitment become material.
- A trust boundary: add a threat model, authentication, and secrets sections.
