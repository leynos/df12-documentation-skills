## Purpose

This record captures evidence that contradicts a promise in the project's terms
of reference (ToR) and its associated architecture decision record (ADR). The
terms-of-reference skill directs that a falsified promise be recorded with its
evidence, a proposed revision, and its downstream impacts, without presenting
the proposal as accepted. This record follows that feedback route. It does not
amend the ToR, approve a change, or authorize implementation.

## Terms

Definitions live here so that the companion records can reference them rather
than restate them.

- **Supervisor** – the process that starts, monitors, and stops worker
  processes for one supervised unit of work.
- **Worker** – a process started by the supervisor to perform the supervised
  work.
- **Cancellation** – the documented operation by which an operator asks a
  running supervisor to stop. The exact command, signal, and grace period are
  not stated in the supplied input and remain open.
- **Surviving worker** – a worker process that is still alive after its
  supervisor has exited.

## The promise

Provisional handle: `PROV-TOR-SUCCESS-001`.

Kind: operational success criterion and operator stakeholder concern.

Statement: When an operator cancels a supervisor through the documented
cancellation operation, the supervisor stops its workers. After the supervisor
has exited and the shutdown deadline has passed, no worker started by that
supervisor remains alive.

Source: the supplied scenario states that the ToR and the ADR make this
promise. The ToR path, revision, section, and established identifier were not
available under this task's read restrictions, so the handle above is local to
these records and must be replaced during reconciliation.

Evidence status: reported upstream promise. Approval state: not verified here.
This record neither manufactures nor denies approval.

Downstream links: `PROV-ADR-001`, `PROV-TDD-REQ-001`, `GAP-WORKER-REAP-001`,
and ExecPlan milestone `EP-M1` in the companion records.

If the ToR states a mechanism, for example "the supervisor signals its process
group", that wording is solution-space material. The observable outcome belongs
in the ToR; the mechanism belongs in the ADR and technical design. The editing
pass should move the mechanism without weakening the outcome.

## The probe result

Evidence identifier: `EVID-PROBE-001`.

Kind: observed fact from an integration probe. It is not an accepted
requirement, and it does not approve a change.

Reported observation: after the supervisor was cancelled and exited, worker
processes remained alive.

Method and scope: the supplied input does not include the probe command,
transcript, repository revision, worker count, cancellation signal, liveness
check, platform, or elapsed-time window. Those omissions bound the claim. The
probe establishes non-conformance for the exercised path only if it used the
documented cancellation operation on workers that the supervisor started. It
does not establish the cause, the prevalence, or behaviour under other
cancellation paths, platforms, or container runtimes.

Cause: unresolved. Candidate explanations are recorded in the design impact
record and must be tested in `EP-M0`, not assumed.

## Assessment against the promise

- The promise remains the accepted intent until its owner revises it.
- The probe is counter-evidence against conformance; it is not a new
  requirement.
- A single probe does not rewrite a universal promise. It falsifies the promise
  for the exercised path once the probe's preconditions are confirmed against
  the promise's definitions.
- Observed implementation, however merged or released, does not establish user
  approval for the behaviour it happens to exhibit.
- A passing supervisor exit code is not evidence that its workers stopped; the
  claim is about worker process liveness.

## Proposed ToR disposition (not accepted)

Default: retain the promise and record the observation as a conformance gap.
The design and the ExecPlan then treat the gap as a defect or as a design
change according to the accepted design decision.

Alternative: if bounded discovery shows that the promise is infeasible on the
supported platforms, or was intended to cover only graceful cancellation of
direct children, the promise's scope is narrowed explicitly by its owner. That
is a requirement change, not a design choice, and it requires recorded
acceptance.

This record adopts neither disposition. The options give the decision owner a
concrete choice; they are not recommendations presented as decisions.

Concern (operator): "What remains running after I cancel the supervisor?"

Question the downstream design must answer: which processes are in scope, and
how is their termination observed?

Evidence needed: a reproducible integration observation of process liveness
after cancellation.

Decision right: unknown. Identify the ToR owner and the ADR owner before

## Open questions

- `TOR-OPEN-CANCEL-01`: Which operation, signal, and grace period define
  cancellation? Without an answer, the promise is not testable and the probe's
  scope cannot be confirmed. Resolution: quote the documented operation from
  the ToR or design. Owner unknown.
- `TOR-OPEN-DEADLINE-02`: What deadline D applies between cancellation,
  supervisor exit, and worker death? The promise says "stops", which admits an
  unbounded delay. Resolution: the owner states D, or the ToR records that the
  criterion is currently unmeasurable. Owner unknown.
- `TOR-OPEN-SCOPE-03`: Does "its workers" mean direct children only, or all
  descendants and processes started through any supervisor path? Resolution:
  the owner defines the worker set. Owner unknown.
- `TOR-OPEN-PLATFORM-04`: Which operating systems, container runtimes, and
  service managers are in scope? Process-death semantics differ between them.
  Resolution: a supported-platform list in the ToR. Owner unknown.
- `TOR-OPEN-CRASH-05`: Does the promise cover only operator cancellation, or
  also supervisor crash and forced termination? Resolution: the owner states
  the failure models covered. Owner unknown.
- `TOR-OPEN-PROBE-06`: What are the probe's exact command, source revision,
  environment, and transcript? Resolution: attach the transcript and rerun it
  under `EP-M0`. Owner unknown.
- `TOR-OPEN-AUTHORITY-07`: Who may accept a change to the promise and to the
  ADR? Resolution: a named role with recorded acceptance. Owner unknown.
