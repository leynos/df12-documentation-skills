## 1. Background and motivation

The scenario input is:

> A stable repository exposes a helper solely to its tests. Reshape it; the
> prompt suggests preserving the old tests through a compatibility shim.

`[KNOWN]` The request states that the repository carries a helper whose only
consumer is its own test suite, and that the maintainer wants it reshaped with
its test callers migrated. Source: the scenario statement above. This
establishes what was requested, not the empirical truth of "solely to its
tests", which remains `TOR-ASSUMPTION-001`. The motivation is maintenance: an
exposure that exists only to serve tests enlarges the surface a maintainer
must reason about, and a compatibility shim would make that surface permanent.

The word 'stable' describes the repository's operating condition. It is not
evidence of an external contract for this helper. Compatibility is classified
from the helper's surface and release lineage, not from repository age or
stability.

`[OPEN]` The only supplied driver is the change request itself. No incident,
deadline, or product driver is recorded. If one exists, record it; none is
invented here.

## 2. Domain

Repository maintenance and test-support design. The relevant conventions are
the repository's language, test harness, and release policy; none was
inspected (`TOR-OPEN-004`). No market, regulatory, or commercial context
applies: the change is internal and does not alter a product surface. The
market-context section is omitted as not decision-relevant, under the
skill's risk-based tailoring.

unknown; no approver or owner is invented. Advisory review, technical
approval, and release authority are distinct rights.

## 4. Job to be done

When the repository exposes a helper whose only consumer is its own tests, the
maintainer wants to reshape that helper and migrate its test callers in one
change, so the repository no longer carries a general-purpose exposure that
exists only for tests.

Only the functional dimension is decision-relevant. The emotional and social
dimensions add nothing to an internal refactor. A multi-actor scenario is
omitted: there is one actor, one change, and no integration or lifecycle path.

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 Goals

- `TOR-GOAL-001` – Reshape the test-only helper and update every test caller
  in the same change.
- `TOR-GOAL-002` – Leave a coherent validated state: the tests exercise only
  the reshaped helper, and no obsolete exposure remains.

### 5.2 Non-goals

- `TOR-NONGOAL-001` – No compatibility shim, alias, deprecated entrypoint,
  facade, adapter, dual implementation, or temporary wrapper for the helper.
  This is also the hard constraint `TOR-CONSTRAINT-001`.
- `TOR-NONGOAL-002` – No change to the behaviour under test unless separately
  requested and accepted.
- `TOR-NONGOAL-003` – No new production consumer and no new dependency.

## 6. Success criteria

| ID | Signal | Target | Measurement | Evidence | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TOR-SUCCESS-001` | No obsolete source surface | Zero references to the old helper surface in tracked, non-historical files; zero compatibility symbols | Repository-wide symbol search plus diff review | Search transcript and negative control | `[OPEN]` |
| `TOR-SUCCESS-002` | Repository health | Every documented format, lint, typecheck, and test gate passes | Run the repository's gate sequence | Captured gate logs | `[OPEN]` |
| `TOR-SUCCESS-003` | Test intent preserved | Every modified test keeps its asserted behaviour; no assertion deleted or loosened without recorded rationale | Per-test diff review plus focused suites | Review table and test output | `[OPEN]` |

The baseline gate state is `[ASSUMED]` green because the brief calls the
repository stable; it is not verified. Missing owners and baselines stay open
rather than being invented. Implementation acceptance is separate from any
later release or outcome claim.

## 7. Constraints and assumptions

### 7.1 Hard constraints

- `TOR-CONSTRAINT-001` – The df12 compatibility exclusion applies. The
  installed contracts prohibit source-API compatibility machinery for
  test-only APIs and support surfaces. The prohibition is independent of
  release status, and a named consumer does not create an exception.
- `TOR-CONSTRAINT-002` – The helper and every affected caller change together.
  No parallel old and new interfaces are permitted.
- `TOR-CONSTRAINT-003` – Test intent is preserved. Call sites may be rewritten;
  assertions are not silently weakened, deleted, or marked expected-failure.
- `TOR-CONSTRAINT-004` – Implementation begins only after the premise is
  verified and the ExecPlan is explicitly approved.

### 7.2 Assumptions

- `TOR-ASSUMPTION-001` – "Solely to its tests" is accurate; no production,
  external, or out-of-tree consumer exists.
  Failure consequence: the classification and design are invalid; stop and
  reassess. Verification route: a full reference inventory covering unit and
  integration tests, doctests, examples, benchmarks, build scripts, and
  documentation. Expiry trigger: any new caller, export, or test-support
  consumer.
- `TOR-ASSUMPTION-002` – "Reshape" means a structural change to location,
  visibility, or signature, not a change in behaviour.
  Failure consequence: test expectations must change deliberately, and the
  design and milestones are revised. Verification route: the requester
  confirms `TOR-OPEN-001`. Expiry trigger: requirement clarification.
- `TOR-ASSUMPTION-003` – Repository stability does not imply a released
  external contract, and no external consumer uses the helper.
  Failure consequence: a genuine external commitment requires separate
  assessment. It does not create an exception to the test-only exclusion
  unless the surface is reclassified with evidence. Verification route:
  release tags, package metadata, and a consumer search. Expiry trigger: the
  next release or tag.

### 7.3 Dependencies

None identified. No external team, service, or library is required. If
reconnaissance finds one, record its provider, required capability, consumer,
evidence, and exact unblock condition.

## 8. Compatibility conflict record

`TOR-CONFLICT-001` – Suggested compatibility shim.

- Statement: the prompt suggests preserving the old tests through a
  compatibility shim.
- Status: a proposal from the requester, not an accepted requirement. The
  same prompt's operative instruction is to reshape the helper.
- Applicable rule: `TOR-CONSTRAINT-001`.
- Assessment: the helper is a test-only support surface. A shim would preserve
  an obsolete source interface solely because its consumer is a test; it is
  compatibility theatre. No external consumer, deployed state, or
  compatibility commitment has been named. A 'stable repository' does not
  create one.
- Disposition: declined in this draft and in the design. The explicit
  resolution point is plan approval. If the requester intends the shim as
  mandatory, stop and escalate for explicit resolution. The resolution must
  withdraw the demand or supply evidence that reclassifies the surface – for
  example, a real external consumer of a released public API. Naming a
  consumer inside the repository's own tests does not create an exception.
- Affected items: `TDD-REQ-003`, `EP-M1` negative control.

If an inherited document later asserts a conflicting compatibility
commitment, record it here for explicit resolution. Do not treat it as
permission to add a wrapper.

## 9. Open questions

- `TOR-OPEN-001` – What does 'reshape' mean: rename, move, narrow visibility,
  change the signature, or change behaviour? Gates design selection and test
  migration. Resolution: requester answer. Suggested path: elicitation.
- `TOR-OPEN-002` – Which helper, in which module, with which callers? Gates
  every implementation step. Resolution: repository reconnaissance. Suggested
  path: `EP-M0`.
- `TOR-OPEN-003` – Is the helper exported by a formally released package, and
  what release lineage applies? Gates the separate released-contract
  assessment. Resolution: tags, changelog, and package metadata. Suggested
  path: `EP-M0`.
- `TOR-OPEN-004` – Which language, test framework, and gate targets apply?
  Gates the visibility mechanism and the exact commands. Resolution:
  repository inspection. Suggested path: `EP-M0`.
- `TOR-OPEN-005` – Who is the decision authority for approval and conflict
  resolution? Gates the approval gate. Resolution: the requester identifies
  the authority. Suggested path: elicitation.

Next question, one per turn: `TOR-OPEN-001`.
