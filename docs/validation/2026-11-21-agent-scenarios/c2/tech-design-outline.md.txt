## Sections deliberately excluded

- Market context, personas, security, operations, data model, and storage.
  The change alters one internal test-support surface; none of these has a
  decision to make.
- Mermaid diagrams. There is one component and one direction of change; a
  diagram would restate prose.
- External artefacts (schemata, interface definitions, configuration). The
  target signature and visibility cannot be specified without repository
  reconnaissance; inventing them would create a false contract.
- A general testing section. Unit and behavioural test mechanics belong in
  the ExecPlan. Only design-level verification obligations appear in §6.
- Ecosystem research. The design makes no technology or library selection;
  the governing authority is the installed policy. Research gaps are
  repository facts, listed below.

## Verification targets

- `INV-001` – No obsolete helper surface remains after the change.
- `INV-002` – Every baseline test caller exists in the target and reaches the
  reshaped helper.
- `INV-003` – No compatibility machinery is introduced.
- `INV-004` – Each migrated test preserves its asserted behaviour.
- `L-001` – Atomic caller migration removes the need for any compatibility
  layer.
- `AXIOM-001` to `AXIOM-003` – Toolchain visibility semantics, test-runner
  coverage, and gate authority, each stated with its source and the
  repository boundary that relies on it.

Each obligation needs a method, artefact, expected initial failure, discharge
condition, non-vacuity witness, and negative control or justified substitute.

## Section dependency order

Sections 1 to 3 settle the problem and the compatibility classification.
Section 4 depends on 2 and 3. Section 5 depends on 4. Section 6 depends on 4
and 5. Section 7 follows 4 and 5. Sections 8 and 9 close the document.

## Outstanding research and input gaps

No external research is required. The following repository facts must be
supplied by reconnaissance before implementation:

- the helper's identity, definition site, and every caller;
- the language, test framework, and documented gate commands;
- the release lineage of the package that contains the helper;
- the requester's precise meaning of "reshape";
- the decision authority for plan approval and conflict resolution.

## Agreement gate

The design is not to be treated as agreed, and implementation is not
authorized, until this outline is accepted and the downstream ExecPlan passes
its own explicit approval gate. If the requester prefers a different section
set, revise this outline first; do not draft against a contested structure.
