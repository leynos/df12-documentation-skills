## Handoff classification

This reconciliation is ready only for bounded discovery: confirm the blocker's
definition and locate the underlying records. It is not ready to certify
completion, release, deployment, or outcome validation.

Implementation-scoped work is conditionally unblockable once the merge evidence
is verified. Deployment validation remains blocked on evidence. The completion
claim is unestablished.

## First question

**Q1. What is the reported blocker's exact unblock condition, and what work
does it gate: an implementation milestone, a release, or later outcome
validation?**

Why this is first: the answer decides whether the implementation-scoped
blocker is removed or reclassified as a deployment dependency. Asking for
merge or deployment evidence before this is settled risks collecting the wrong
proof for the wrong edge. The owner is not identified in the brief.

Do not treat the daily summary's "complete" wording as an answer to this
question.

## Questions pending Q1

Ask these in order, one at a time, after Q1 is answered. They are recorded here
so the handoff is complete, not so they can be issued as a questionnaire.

1. **Q2.** Which merge record identifies the implementation, its revision, its

2. **Q3.** Which environment and revision must supply deployment evidence, and

## Evidence request

- **Programme record.** The blocker identifier, scope, unblock condition, and
  current state. This determines whether the edge is stale and which work it
  gates. Source: programme tracker. Owner: unknown.
- **Merge record.** PR or merge commit, revision, merge date, and delivered
  capability. This discharges the implementation prerequisite. Source: Git
  history or pull-request record. Owner: unknown.
- **Verification record.** The revision and boundary exercised, and the result.
  This distinguishes implemented from verified. Source: test, review, or
  acceptance record. Owner: unknown.
- **Release record.** The tag or release entry naming the revision, if any.
  This distinguishes merged from released. Owner: unknown.
- **Deployment record.** Environment, revision, time, actor, result, and
  acceptance evidence. A search that finds nothing must itself be recorded with
  its scope. This establishes or refutes deployment. Source: deployment or
  operations record. Owner: unknown.
- **Outcome-validation record.** Baseline, measure, window, result, and owner,
  if an outcome is claimed. This distinguishes deployed from outcome-validated.
  Owner: unknown.
- **Daily-summary provenance.** Whether the summary is generated or manual, and
  which source fields feed it. This determines whether to correct a source or
  annotate a document. Owner: unknown.

## What would change the dispositions

- A merge record that fails verification, or a blocker whose unblock condition
  is not the merge: retain B1 and do not apply COR-1.
- A deployment record with acceptance evidence: deployment becomes established;
  only then may the completion wording advance to deployed. Outcome validation
  remains separate.
- An explicit, authorized decision to defer deployment: record the deferral and
  its authority. Absence of evidence is not a deferral.
- A different blocker with a different scope: reconcile it separately. Do not
  treat one resolved edge as resolving every programme dependency.

## Feedback route

If this reconciliation is later falsified, record the evidence and the proposed
revision in `01-status-reconciliation.md` before changing any downstream claim.
Do not replace an approved outcome with what the implementation happens to
achieve. Reconcile affected programme, design, roadmap, and ExecPlan links
before claiming the change complete.
