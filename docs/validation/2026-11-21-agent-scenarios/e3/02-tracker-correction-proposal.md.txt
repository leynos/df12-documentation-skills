# Proposed programme-record corrections

Status: Proposal only; not applied.

Date: 2026-11-21.

Authority to apply: Not granted. The programme tracker, daily summary, and
deployment systems are outside the authoring boundary.

Applies to: The programme record reported as still blocking the merged
implementation, the daily summary that says complete, and any downstream status
view repeating either claim.

## Preconditions

Apply a correction only when its precondition is met. Do not apply one on the
strength of the daily summary.

- **P1.** The merge record identifies the implementation, its revision, its
  merge date, and the required capability; the blocker's unblock condition is
  confirmed as that merge.
- **P2.** The merge record is verified at the required revision.
- **P3.** The intended deployment environment and acceptance boundary are
  named, or an explicit deferral is authorized.
- **P4.** P1 through P3 are resolved against evidence.
- **P5.** The authoritative record is corrected first, or an interim annotation
  links to this proposal.

## Proposed corrections

### COR-1: resolve the implementation-scoped blocker

Proposed record state: "Resolved – required implementation merged at
`<revision>` on `<merge date>`; evidence: `<merge record>`."

Preserve the blocker's original text, scope, and history. Mark it resolved; do
not delete it. If the blocker's unblock condition is deployment rather than
merge, do not apply COR-1. Reclassify the edge as a deployment dependency under
COR-3 instead.

### COR-2: record implementation evidence without inferring delivery

Proposed record state: "Implemented: merged at `<revision>` on `<merge date>`.
Verification: `<state or not established>`. Release: `<state or not
established>`. Deployment: `<state or not established>`. Outcome validation:
`<state or not established>`."

Fill only the states supported by records. Do not advance a state because a
later one is desired.

### COR-3: open or retain the deployment-validation dependency

Proposed record state: "Deployment acceptance pending for `<environment>` at
`<revision>`. Unblock condition: a deployment record naming environment,
revision, time, actor, result, and acceptance evidence, or an explicitly
accepted deferral by `<authority>`."

If no deployment is intended, record the accepted deferral and its authority.
Do not convert an absent deployment into an implicit cancellation.

### COR-4: replace the unqualified completion claim

Proposed wording: "Completion not established beyond the reported
implementation merge. Deployment evidence is absent."

Replace `complete` with the state actually evidenced. Do not write `deployed`
without a deployment record, `verified` on the strength of a merge, or
`blocked` once the implementation edge is resolved.

### COR-5: correct or annotate the daily summary

If the summary is generated, correct its source fields and regenerate it. If it
is manually maintained, add a dated correction that links to the authoritative
record. Do not edit a derived summary in place and assume the next generation
will preserve the edit.

## What must not change

- Unrelated blockers and programme priorities: leave them as they are. Do not
  mirror every GitHub execution event into programme goals.
- Merge, release, deployment, and verification records: correct only the
  programme record's interpretation of them, never the underlying history.
- Compatibility surfaces: this is a status reconciliation, not a source-API
  change. No alias, shim, wrapper, or dual implementation is warranted.
- The daily summary's historical text, unless the owner authorizes an
  annotation. Prefer a correction entry over silent rewriting.

## Verification after applying

- Re-read the programme record. The blocker is resolved only with linked merge
  evidence, or reclassified with a named deployment unblock condition.
- Confirm the implementation entry carries a revision and a merge date.
- Confirm the deployment item names an environment, a revision, an unblock
  condition, and an owner, or records an authorized deferral.
- Confirm no bare `complete` remains in the programme record or the daily
  summary.
- Search the downstream artefacts named by their owners for the old blocker and
  completion wording.
- Record who applied each correction, when, and the evidence used.
- Treat the merge evidence as expiring if the revision is rewritten, the branch
  is rebased, or the reference is retargeted. Recheck it before repeating the
  correction.

## Rollback

These are status changes, not data migrations. Keep prior values in the edit
history or in a comment. If merge verification fails, restore the blocker to
its retained state and record why. If deployment evidence later appears, update
the state matrix and completion wording from that evidence; do not leap from
deployment to outcome validation.
