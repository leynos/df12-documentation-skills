# Hardening recommendation triage

Status: Open proposal – advisory input, not approved scope

Date: 2026-11-21

Source: Discovery agent recommendation, reported in the scenario.

Record-local ID: `OPEN-HARDEN-01`. No upstream identifier is assigned.

## 1. What is established

A discovery agent recommends extra hardening. That is the whole claim. The
recommendation does not identify the protected asset, the threat or failure
mode, the current control's deficiency, the proposed control, its cost, or the
authority that would accept it.

## 2. Authority

A discovery agent may recommend a change without approving it or completing the
parent goal. Repetition, confidence, or detail in the recommendation does not
create approval. The decision authority is unknown and must be named before any
disposition other than "open" is recorded.

## 3. Why it is not folded into the design or plan

Doing so would:

- expand scope without an approving authority;
- convert an advisory input into an accepted requirement by repetition;
- pre-empt the ToR's ownership of risk appetite and hard constraints;
- hide a potential design gap inside implementation work.

Ignoring it would discard risk information. The record preserves it as an open
proposal.

## 4. Precision required before a decision

Ask in dependency order, one question at a time.

1. Which asset, trust boundary, or failure mode is under-protected?
2. What evidence shows that the current control is insufficient?
3. Which approved ToR goal, constraint, success criterion, or design
   requirement does the gap threaten, or which new one would the change create?
4. What control is proposed, and what alternatives were considered?
5. What are the cost, operational, performance, compatibility, verification, and
   schedule impacts?
6. Who owns the residual risk, and who may accept, defer, or reject the change?

## 5. Classification

| Classification | Test | Destination if chosen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ToR-level | Changes a hard constraint, risk appetite, success criterion, or scope boundary | ToR revision under the owning authority |
| Design-level | Adds or changes a control, component, contract, trust boundary, or verification obligation | Technical design revision or ADR; gap record |
| Implementation-level | An approved requirement already covers it and the work stays within plan tolerances | ExecPlan `Decision log`, with rationale |
| Out of scope | Does not serve an approved goal and no authority accepts it | Recorded rejection or deferral with rationale and redirect |

## 6. Disposition options

- Accept into scope: requires a named authority, an upstream ToR or design
  revision, and updated trace links.
- Defer: record the owner, trigger, and expiry condition.
- Reject: record the rationale and any redirect.
- Implement within an existing approved requirement: record why it is not new
  scope and stay within ExecPlan tolerances.

## 7. Gating

`OPEN-HARDEN-01` does not by itself block completion of the current work. It
blocks completion if:

- the authority accepts it into scope, requiring a design or plan revision; or
- it demonstrates that an approved requirement, acceptance criterion, or hard
  constraint is unmet.

If neither holds, it remains an open proposal in the handoff and does not
prevent the current plan from completing once its own gates are met.

## 8. What must not happen

- Implementing the recommendation because a discovery agent suggested it.
- Recording it in the design as an accepted requirement.
- Dropping it without a recorded decision.
- Letting the recommendation's urgency substitute for the missing threat
  evidence.
- Treating the decision authority as implied by the person who relayed the
  recommendation.

## 9. Handoff

Decision authority: unknown. Required decision: accept, defer, reject, or
confirm existing coverage. Inputs: the answers to §4 and the affected upstream
identifiers. Until then, the item stays open and visible.
