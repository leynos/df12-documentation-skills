## 11. Outcomes and retrospective (interim)

- Outcome: execution stopped at the milestone boundary. No completion is
  claimed, and no design or verification state is certified.
- Lesson: an approval decision and its authoritative upstream record are
  separate states. A plan's `Conformance basis` is invalid as soon as an
  approved architectural decision is missing from the design or ADR.
- Remaining: every item in the reconciliation gate, followed by resumed
  execution and re-verification.
