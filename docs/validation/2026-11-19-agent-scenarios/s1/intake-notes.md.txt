## Tailoring decisions

- Keep the terms of reference short: a small number of identifiers, no
  stakeholder zoo, no manufactured non-goals, and no ceremonial enterprise
  domains. This follows the risk-based tailoring in
  `problem-contract.md` ("a small library may need only a few IDs").
- Do not draft a technical design or an ExecPlan. The terms of reference is
  v0.1 with its substantive content unelicited. `tech-design-doc` requires an
  agreed upstream basis, and `execplans` requires an approved design slice and
  an explicit approval gate. Neither exists.
- Do not record compatibility machinery. The library is greenfield, pre-1.0 by
  construction, has no released 1.0+ contract, and names no external consumer.
  Nothing warrants an alias, shim, facade, or migration wrapper.
- Do not invent competitors, users, goals, success measures, owners, decision
  rights, or approvals. Each unknown is recorded as an open question or left
  explicitly unresolved.

## Conspicuous absences (elicitation targets)

- Problem statement, domain, and field of practice.
- Primary and non-users, and any stakeholder beyond the work's originator.
- Job to be done, goals, non-goals, and success signals.
- The meaning of "small" in scope terms.
- Any constraint beyond the two recorded: licence, supported platforms,
  budget, deadline, and operational expectations.
- Identity and decision rights of the brief's author or approver.
