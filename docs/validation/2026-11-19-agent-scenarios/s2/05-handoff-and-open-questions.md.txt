## Proposed terms-of-reference revision

No terms-of-reference revision is asserted. The ToR skill requires proposed
changes to be recorded without being presented as accepted. The sections that
may need revision, pending OQ-4, are:

- **Assumptions.** If the change falsifies an assumption, record the failure
  consequence and expiry trigger, and update the evidence status.
- **Hard constraints.** If the change conflicts with a constraint, record the
  conflict and its resolution; do not relax the constraint to fit the
  implementation.
- **Success criteria.** If the change alters a measurable outcome, record a
  proposed revision with its baseline and owner; do not substitute a cheaper
  metric.
- **Open questions.** Add any unresolved item that the design cannot decide.

The corresponding decision authority is the ToR owner and, for scope changes,
the sponsor. A discovery recommendation does not qualify as either.

## Completion state

This record has not reached a completion state, and it must not be reported as
complete. The reasons are structural, not editorial:

1. An upstream architectural change is unrecorded in the design and ADR set.
   The reconciliation rule forbids `COMPLETE` while an upstream change or
   deviation remains unrecorded or unaccepted.
2. The hardening recommendation has no approving authority, so it cannot be
   closed by the reconciler.
3. The approving decision for IN-2 belongs to a human role that has not acted
   in this session. Silence is not approval, and a drafted record is not an
   accepted one.

The correct current status is `BLOCKED` for the plan and `proposed` for the
design change and ADR. A passing audit, a merged change, or a green gate would
not change either status.
