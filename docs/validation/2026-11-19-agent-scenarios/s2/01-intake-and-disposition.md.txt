## Disposition of IN-1, the hardening recommendation

IN-1 is a reviewer suggestion. Per the architecture contract, it is input, not
an approval and not a licence to widen scope. It is also solution-space
material: a hardening mechanism belongs in the design or an ADR, not in the
terms of reference as a new goal. Turning every audit suggestion into a
strategic goal, or widening a release gate to accommodate it, requires
authorization this session does not have.

Disposition: recorded as `GAP-HARDEN-001`, status `proposed work, not
authorized`. The available responses are:

1. Accept it into the design as a new requirement or design element, with a
   named approving authority and a recorded requirement or accepted-risk
   statement.
2. Reject or defer it, recording the residual risk and the owner who accepted
   that risk.
3. Fund bounded discovery to establish whether the proposed hardening addresses
   a real trust-boundary weakness, without treating discovery as approval.

It does not enter the terms of reference as a goal. If the recommendation
reveals a genuine gap against an existing hard constraint or success criterion,
the correct move is to record that gap and ask the constraint's owner, not to
rewrite the goal to match the suggestion.

## Disposition of IN-2, the approved architectural change

IN-2 is different in kind. An approved architectural decision already exists in
some record that is not the design or the ADR set. Propagation, not invention,
is the action. Two branches must be distinguished before any prose is changed:

1. If the approval record is produced, reconciled, and current, the design and
   ADR are stale records of an accepted decision. The remedy is a design change
   request, an ADR candidate, impact analysis in both directions, and an
   ExecPlan revision. The ExecPlan remains `BLOCKED` until that accepted state
   is propagated, not until the architecture is re-approved.
2. If no approval record can be produced, IN-2 is an unapproved architectural
   deviation. The ExecPlan records the proposed deviation, identifies affected
   identifiers, enters `BLOCKED`, and awaits explicit acceptance. It does not
   proceed on the implementation's say-so.

This session cannot decide between those branches. The record therefore states
both and marks the branch selection `[OPEN]`.
