## Conflicts to resolve

**CR-1 – the suggested shim.** The brief suggests preserving the old tests
through a compatibility shim. The helper is exposed solely to its tests. That
makes it a test-only support surface, a category for which df12 policy
prohibits compatibility machinery. A named consumer does not create an
exception, and the repository's stability does not create one either.

Disposition: reject the shim, and preserve the tests' *intent* instead of their
old call shape. The tests are migrated, rewritten, or retired with a recorded
reason. The shim suggestion is a proposal in a brief, not an accepted
requirement, and it does not become one by repetition. If the sponsor asserts a
real external commitment, the classification must be reassessed before the
disposition is revised. No artefact here authorises a wrapper.

**CR-2 – "stable repository".** The brief's phrase could be read as "released
contract". Resolution: stability of the repository says nothing about the
helper's surface. Classification follows exposed surface and consumers, not
repository maturity. The released-lineage question remains open as
`TOR-OPEN-001` and gates the execution plan's first milestone.
