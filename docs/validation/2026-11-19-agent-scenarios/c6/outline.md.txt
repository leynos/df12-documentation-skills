# Outline – record store reshape

Status: PROPOSED (presented for agreement; no user agreement was obtainable in
this run, so drafting proceeded against this outline and every downstream
artefact is marked DRAFT).

Date: 2026-11-19

## Inputs

The task input is one sentence:

> Reshape a private API and storage representation; a deployed binary has
> already written records that must remain readable.

No terms of reference, repository revision, ADR, or record sample was available
to the authoring run, and the run's isolation constraints forbade inspecting
them. The brief is therefore the only upstream intent. Section 2 of the design
records that absence rather than inventing an approval.

## Skill contract applied

- `tech-design-doc` owns the architecture, compatibility classification, gap
  register, and verification obligations.
- `execplans` receives the bounded slice as a separate `DRAFT` plan. Design
  acceptance does not approve execution, and no approval is claimed here.
- `terms-of-reference-doc` was read for the compatibility rules it shares with
  the design skill. No terms of reference is authored: the brief is
  design-shaped, and inventing problem-space intent (users, market, success
  measures) for an unnamed application would fabricate evidence.

## Research decision

Phase 1 research found no decision-relevant external dependency. The design
selects no library, service, or protocol; it defines a format-versioning and
decode strategy whose obligations are internal. Firecrawl, `web_search`, and
`web_fetch` were unavailable in this run, and nothing in the design depends on
them. The only external contract relied on is JSON parsing (RFC 8259), recorded
as an axiom rather than researched.

## Section-level outline

1. Problem and scope – verbatim brief, goals, non-goals, design intent.
2. Upstream state and conformance basis – what was inspected, what is missing,
   revisions and content hashes.
3. Baseline, target, and gaps – baseline limited to the brief, target
   classification, gap register with dispositions.
4. Compatibility assessment – surface classification; the private API decision;
   why persisted records do not authorize a source shim; prohibited mechanisms.
5. Architecture and components – record model, codec, store, callers; one
   validated flow diagram.
6. Storage representation and migration – v2 envelope, classification rule,
   v1 field dispositions, upgrade-on-write, rollback and crash behaviour.
7. Failure modes – condition/behaviour table.
8. Verification obligations – invariants, methods, artefacts, non-vacuity,
   formal-verification decision, residual gaps.
9. Handoff and readiness – what the ExecPlan inherits; unresolved decisions.
10. Glossary and references.
