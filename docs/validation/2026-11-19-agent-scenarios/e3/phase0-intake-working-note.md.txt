## What each artefact is authoritative for

A1 is authoritative for the programme record's current assertion. It is not
authoritative for whether the gated work is still blocked.

A2, once its record is inspected, establishes what implementation landed. It
cannot establish verification, release, deployment, or an outcome.

A3 is an evidence gap. It is not evidence of absence and it does not license
an inference in either direction.

A4 is a discovery aid. It points at records to inspect; it does not certify
them. Generated summaries and agent reports do not approve themselves by
repetition.

## Conflicts and gaps

- C1: A1 reports a block, while A2 reports that the gated implementation
  merged. Either the block is stale or it gates something other than the
  implementation. Resolve against the blocker's stated unblock condition, not
  against the more recent or more convenient artefact.
- C2: A4 reports complete, while A3 is absent. "Complete" is ambiguous across
  proposed, approved, implemented, verified, released, deployed, and
  outcome-validated. The claim selects none of them.
- C3: no artefact states whether the blocker gates a design decision, an
  implementation milestone, a release, or outcome validation.
- G1: no revision is pinned for A2.
- G2: no owner or decision authority is named for A1 or A4.
- G3: no definition of acceptable deployment evidence is supplied.
- G4: no acceptance evidence exists for any state beyond the reported merge.
